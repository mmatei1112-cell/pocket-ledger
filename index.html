Apple Pay auto-log

When you pay by tapping your iPhone or Watch, a Shortcut sends the amount and shop to a Google Sheet that only you own. Pocket Ledger adds them every time you open it.

On your computer

  1. Create a new Google Sheet at sheets.new, then open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete the code that's there and paste the Pocket Ledger script.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment, choose Web app, set Who has access to Anyone, then click Deploy.
  4. Allow access when Google asks. If it says the app isn't verified, click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe). It's your own script.
  5. Copy the Web app URL and send it to your phone.

On your iPhone, in Shortcuts

  1. Open Automation, tap + and choose Transaction (called Wallet on newer iOS).
  2. Pick your cards, keep all categories, choose Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run. Tap Next, then New Blank Automation.
  3. Add the action Get Contents of URL and paste the same Web app link.
  4. Tap the arrow on the action. Set Method to POST and Request Body to JSON.
  5. Add three Text fields: amount, merchant and card. For each value, pick Shortcut Input, tap it again and choose Amount, Merchant and Card or Pass.
  6. Tap Done. Your next Apple Pay payment shows up here with an Apple Pay tag.

Only taps with Apple Pay are picked up. Paying with the plastic card, online checkouts and bank transfers still need adding by hand. Payments abroad are logged as the amount shown, in your app's currency.
